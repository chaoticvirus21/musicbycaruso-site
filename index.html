About Frank

A lifetime with a guitar

Frank Caruso has spent a lifetime with a guitar in hand, playing the classic rock and folk songs that get a whole room singing along. Based in Central Florida, he performs at venues, private events, and community gatherings across the region.

Whether it's an intimate solo set or a full band, every show is built around one idea: the right song at the right time. Reach out and let's talk about the music for your next event.
